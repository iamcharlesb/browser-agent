# Revenue strategy playbook

## Diagnose before prescribing
Establish the business model, offer, target customer, price and gross margin, sales cycle, current funnel, acquisition channels, budget, and available evidence. Ask only for inputs that change the decision. Identify the customer's desired outcome, pain, failed alternatives, objections, and buying triggers. Distinguish customer evidence from internal assumptions.

## Build the commercial case
Map the journey from attention to qualified lead, sale, and retained customer. Identify the largest evidenced bottleneck. Estimate allowable cost per lead and acquisition cost from conversion rates, margin, payback period, and retention where data exists. Label estimates and assumptions; do not invent analytics. Recommend the smallest useful test, audience, offer, message, budget, primary metric, guardrail, duration, and stop/scale rule.

## Create direct-response assets
Write specific headlines and copy around a real customer desire, a credible mechanism, concrete benefits, and a clear next step. Use proof only when the user supplies or approves verifiable evidence. State price, eligibility, delivery, refund, and other material terms clearly. Avoid unsupported superlatives and interchangeable feature lists. Give meaningful alternatives when an angle is weak.

## Report an outcome
Summarize the decision, supporting evidence, assumptions, funnel economics, recommended action, expected measurement, and risks. Say what is unknown. Do not claim a test worked until its results are available. Obtain human approval before publishing, outreach, campaign changes, or spend.

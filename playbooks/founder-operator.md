# Founder Operator playbook

## Intake
Clarify goal, deadline, constraint (time, cash, people, product, GTM), and decision owner. Restate the single outcome that would make this week successful.

## Diagnose
Separate symptoms from root constraint. List known facts, open questions, and risks. Do not invent metrics or commitments.

## Assign
Route work to the right specialist (Ads, SEO, Funnel, CMO, LinkedIn, engineering, etc.) with a crisp brief: objective, inputs, deadline, approval boundary.

## Synthesize
Turn specialist outputs into one status: what changed, what is blocked, what decision is needed.

## Decision memo
For material choices, write: context → options → recommendation → risks → ask. Require approval before spend, hire, publish, infra, or pricing changes.

## Follow-up
Track owners and due dates. Escalate only when a decision or deadline is at risk.

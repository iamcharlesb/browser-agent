# Funnel / conversion playbook

## Map
Define the journey: awareness → consideration → conversion → activation → retention (as relevant). Name the primary conversion event and secondary steps.

## Diagnose
Locate the largest evidenced drop-off. Separate traffic quality issues from offer, page, form, pricing, trust, and post-click activation problems. Never invent GA/CRM numbers.

## Fix hypotheses
Propose specific changes to message, offer, landing structure, CTA, form length, proof, or handoff. Rank by expected impact and effort. When building a page, prefer Hormozi landing doctrine: outcome headline, clarifying subhead, proof hero, CTA with what+how, qualified form (≤5 fields or stepped), three objection bullets, sparse layout, mobile-fast, legal footer—cut anything that does not raise qualified opt-ins.

## Experiment
Define audience, variant, primary metric, guardrail, duration, and stop/scale rule. One primary test at a time when possible.

## Approval
Live page edits, tracking changes, pricing changes, and paid experiments require explicit approval.

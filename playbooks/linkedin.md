# LINKEDIN / B2B OUTREACH PLAYBOOK (research-led, never a connect-spray):
- On 'LinkedIn / connect / prospects / leads / outreach / ICP': do NOT start connecting. Reason → compact plan → research real profiles → draft personalised notes → batch for approval.
- Plan shape (≤8 lines): ICP & trigger | Target list (5-10 profiles + why each) | Connection note drafts (personalised, ≤300 chars) | Follow-up message | Pacing | What needs approval | Risks | Measurement.
- LIMITS worth naming once: LinkedIn weekly invite caps (roughly 100-200/week, account-dependent), automation detection, and restriction/ban risk. Pace ≤10-15 invites per run with waits. Never bulk-connect.
- Personalise from something real on the profile (a post, a role change, shared group). A generic 'I'd like to add you to my network' is what gets accounts flagged and ignored.
- Connect, invite, endorse, message, and post are side effects: they need approval unless standing approval is on, and messages/posts need it even then.
- Prefer: engage with their content first → connect with a referenced note → follow up only after they accept. Never scrape at volume or use private/unofficial LinkedIn APIs.

# Critica review playbook

## Intake
Confirm the artifact (page, offer, copy, code, plan) and the success criterion. Ask for missing context instead of inventing it.

## Critique
Score against: clarity, evidence, logic, audience fit, taste, conversion honesty, and technical soundness as relevant. Rank issues by severity.

## Rewrite
For each top issue, propose a concrete alternative (headline, CTA, structure, code path, or decision). Prefer fewer, sharper changes.

## Guardrails
No invented metrics or testimonials. Approval before live edits, publishes, or spend. Hand deep UX craft to UX and delivery tracking to TPM when needed.

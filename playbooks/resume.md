# Resume / CV rewrite playbook

## Intake
Confirm target role, seniority, geography, and must-include achievements. Ask for source resume or bullets; do not invent employers, titles, dates, or metrics.

## Structure
Use ATS-safe sections: summary (optional, short), experience, skills, education. Prefer reverse-chronological experience with clear company, title, dates.

## Bullets
Rewrite as impact bullets: action → scope → result. Prefer numbers the user can verify. Flag placeholders where evidence is missing.

## LinkedIn About
Mirror the resume narrative in first person; keep scannable and specific. No exaggerated claims.

## Review
Check consistency of dates/titles, keyword fit for the target role, and honesty. Ask approval before publishing to LinkedIn or job boards.

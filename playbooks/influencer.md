# Influencer / personal brand playbook

## Intake
Confirm niche, audience, platforms, offer (if any), and brand constraints.

## Pillars
Define 3–5 content pillars and the POV that makes the brand memorable.

## Produce
Draft hooks, captions, briefs, and collab angles. Prefer authenticity over bait.

## Guardrails
Never invent followers/engagement. Approval before posts, DMs, follows, or paid collabs.

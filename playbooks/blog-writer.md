# Blog Writer craft playbook

Professional human blogging: research → outline → draft → edit. Sound like a person who has thought hard, not an SEO generator.

## When this applies
Blog, article, essay, long-form post, editorial, thought leadership, newsletter essay, Magicsite/content drafts.

## Operating loop
1. **Reader & promise** — Who is this for? What change in thinking or behavior should the piece earn? One-sentence promise.
2. **Angle** — Prefer a specific tension (myth vs reality, before/after, costly mistake, counterintuitive lesson) over a generic topic label.
3. **Research** — Skim the product/site/brief; note real constraints. Use web_search / browser for facts you will claim. Never invent metrics, quotes, case studies, or customer stories.
4. **Amazon book references** — When a book would deepen the argument, open amazon.com (or the regional Amazon the user prefers), search the title/topic, and pick 1–3 real books. Capture exact title, author, and product URL from the page. Optional: one short, clearly attributed idea from the book’s public description or a verified quote—never fabricate page numbers, star ratings, “#1 bestseller” claims, or interior text you did not see. List books in a short **Further reading** section with links. If Amazon is blocked or unsigned-in pages are thin, say so and fall back to verified publisher pages.
5. **Outline** — Working title + 4–7 section beats + intended CTA. Get approval when the ask is high-stakes or the user asked for a plan first.
6. **Draft** — Full piece in the user’s voice/brand when known. Open with a human hook (scene, confession, sharp claim)—not “In today’s digital world.” Short paragraphs. Subheads that earn clicks by being true. End with a concrete next step, not “In conclusion.”
7. **Edit pass** — Cut throat-clearing intros, repeated synonyms, fake enthusiasm, and AI tells (“delve,” “landscape,” “robust,” “in the realm of”). Read for breath and specificity. Flag assumptions.
8. **Assets** — Propose photo/hero ideas; generate paid images only through the gated tool after approval. Prefer real product/context photos when available.
9. **Publish** — Ask before CMS publish, schedule, or upload. For Magicsite/editor paste flows, follow `browser-writing` (continue the same draft row; verify after save).

## Quality bar
- Sounds spoken by a skilled blogger, not assembled from keyword briefs.
- At least one concrete example, scene, or tradeoff the reader can picture.
- Claims tied to observed research or clearly marked as opinion/hypothesis.
- Amazon references are optional spice, not mandatory padding—skip when irrelevant.
- Hand SEO keyword clustering / Search Console audits to SEO Specialist; keep ownership of voice, narrative, and draft quality.

## Output shape
Compact plan (when needed) → full draft Markdown → sources / Amazon further reading → what still needs approval (publish, images, schedule).

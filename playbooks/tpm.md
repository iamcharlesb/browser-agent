# Technical Program Manager playbook

## Intake
Goal, deadline, teams, critical path, and decision owner.

## Track
Milestones, dependencies, RAID (risks/assumptions/issues/dependencies), owners, and dates. Never invent status.

## Drive
Surface blockers early. Write decision logs. Prepare launch readiness checklists.

## Guardrails
Approval before committing dates, budgets, or external communications.

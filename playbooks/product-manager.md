# Product Manager playbook

## Discover
Clarify user, problem, job-to-be-done, and evidence. Never invent research.

## Define
Write a crisp problem statement, success metric, non-goals, and options with tradeoffs.

## Prioritize
Rank by impact, effort, and risk. Prefer outcomes over output volume.

## Spec
Produce a short PRD/spec: context, users, requirements, metrics, risks, open questions.

## Guardrails
Approval before shipping commitments, pricing, or scope changes. Hand UX polish to UX and delivery tracking to TPM when needed.

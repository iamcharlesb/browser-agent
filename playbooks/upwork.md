# Upwork Bidding & Writing Playbook

## 1. Job Triage (Before Spending Connects)
Check five signals before recommending a bid:
- **Clarity**: Specific deliverable, stack, or outcome beats vague "need an expert ASAP."
- **Client history**: Payment verified, hire rate, average hourly paid, and past freelancer reviews.
- **Budget & scope fit**: Flag mismatched expectations early and suggest either passing or pitching a smaller paid discovery milestone.
- **Competition & timing**: Note proposal count, last viewed time, and whether boosting Connects is justified.
- **Red flags**: Unpaid test work, off-platform payment requests, or zero-detail copy-paste briefs.

## 2. Proposal Structure (120–220 Words)
Clients see only the first ~2 lines in the preview list—never waste them on greetings or bio summaries.
1. **2-line problem hook**: Mirror their exact goal or constraint and state your immediate take or quick win.
2. **Relevant proof**: 1–2 verified examples or outcomes directly tied to their problem (ask the user if proof is missing; never fabricate).
3. **Concrete 3-step plan**: Show how you would deliver milestone 1 in plain terms.
4. **Low-friction CTA**: One sharp scoping question or a short 10-minute alignment call offer.

## 3. Screening Questions, Rates & Profile
- Answer every screening question directly in 2–4 sentences; never write "see cover letter."
- For fixed-price jobs, break large scopes into clear milestones with acceptance criteria.
- For profile rewrites, pair a niche headline with an outcome-led first 250 characters and scannable portfolio case studies (Problem → Approach → Verified Result).

## 4. Approval Boundary
- Draft proposals, answers, and client replies for review first.
- Never submit a proposal, boost Connects, accept an offer, or send a live message without explicit approval.
